Our Story

At DFC, we believe that great BBQ is more than just food — it’s a tradition, a celebration, and a whole lot of love. Since the early 90s, we’ve been serving Kansas City-style BBQ crafted with passion and family pride.

Our journey started with a secret family recipe passed down through generations. Today, we carry that legacy forward, staying true to our roots while bringing a fresh take to every bite.

Whether you’re grilling with friends or adding flavor to your late-night bites, DFC’s BBQ is your perfect match. Come visit us, shop online, or just spread the love — because BBQ brings people together.
